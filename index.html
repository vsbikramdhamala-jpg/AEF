Our Beginning & Vision

Aurora Education Foundation was established in 2018 A.D. with a noble vision to help, guide, and support students in their educational journey. The foundation is dedicated to empowering learners by providing proper direction, encouragement, counseling, and opportunities that can help them achieve their academic and career goals. It believes that every student deserves access to the right guidance and a quality education in order to build a bright and successful future.

The foundation works for the overall development of students by focusing not only on academic progress but also on personal growth, confidence, leadership, and social responsibility. Through its various activities and programs, Aurora Education Foundation aims to inspire students to discover their potential, overcome challenges, and become capable, responsible, and productive members of society. It serves as a supportive platform for students who seek motivation, educational advice, and a clear path toward success.

Our Mission

  • To guide students in choosing the right educational course, institution, and career path according to their interests and abilities.
  • To provide counseling, mentorship, and academic support to help students overcome educational challenges.
  • To assist needy, deserving, and underprivileged students in accessing quality education and better opportunities.
  • To develop students’ skills, confidence, creativity, and leadership qualities for their personal and professional growth.
  • To create awareness about the importance of education and encourage lifelong learning for a better future and a better society.

Visa & Documentation Guidelines

Comprehensive document preparation guidelines to ensure 100% visa success rates.

1. Academic Record

Transcripts, Character Certificates, Provisional/Degree Certificates from Grade 10 to Highest Level.

2. Proficiency Test

IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, or JLPT / TOPIK language test scorecard with necessary band criteria.

3. Financial Support

Bank balances, Property Valuation, Income Source Statements, and Tax Clearance certificates.

4. Passport & Identity

Valid Passport (min 1-year validity), Citizenship ID, Relationship verification documentation.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
How much bank balance is required for visa filing?
Requirements vary by country. For the UK and Australia, you generally need to show tuition fees plus 1 year of living costs (approx. NPR 35–45 Lakhs) held for a specific period.
Can I apply with gap years in my education?
Yes! Education gaps can be accepted if supported with genuine work experience, salary receipts, and official tax/appointment documentation.
