Our Beginning & Vision

Aurora Education Foundation was established in 2016 A.D. with a clear purpose: to guide, support, and empower students throughout their educational journey. We provide informed counselling, practical preparation, and meaningful opportunities that help learners make confident academic and career decisions.

The foundation works for the overall development of students by focusing not only on academic progress but also on personal growth, confidence, leadership, and social responsibility. Through its various activities and programs, Aurora Education Foundation aims to inspire students to discover their potential, overcome challenges, and become capable, responsible, and productive members of society. It serves as a supportive platform for students who seek motivation, educational advice, and a clear path toward success.

Our Mission

  • To guide students in choosing the right educational course, institution, and career path according to their interests and abilities.
  • To provide counseling, mentorship, and academic support to help students overcome educational challenges.
  • To assist needy, deserving, and underprivileged students in accessing quality education and better opportunities.
  • To develop students’ skills, confidence, creativity, and leadership qualities for their personal and professional growth.
  • To create awareness about the importance of education and encourage lifelong learning for a better future and a better society.

Visa & Documentation Guidelines

A clear preparation path for Nepalese students, from profile screening to visa lodgement and pre-departure.

01

Profile screening

Review academics, study gaps, work history, English level, budget, and the best-fit destination.

02

Course and application

Select a suitable course and institution, then prepare and submit a complete university application.

03

Offer and compliance

Meet conditions, pay the required deposit, and obtain CAS, CoE, or the relevant confirmation document.

04

Visa lodgement

Complete the online form, pay the current fee, submit biometrics, and prepare for any interview.

05

Pre-departure

Arrange accommodation, insurance, travel, financial access, and an orientation before departure.

Core document checklist

Prepare clear colour scans and keep the originals available. Exact requirements vary by country, course, and applicant profile.

Valid passport and recent photographs
Citizenship and civil documents
Academic certificates and transcripts
IELTS, PTE, TOEFL, JLPT, or TOPIK result
Offer letter and tuition payment receipt
CAS, CoE, or country-specific enrolment confirmation
Bank statements and source of funds
Sponsor, relationship, income, and tax documents
SOP, study plan, or Genuine Student answers
Employment and gap evidence where applicable
Medical, police, biometrics, or TB documents if requested
Visa fee, health cover, and appointment receipts

Country checkpoints

UKOffer, CAS, funds, English proof, tuberculosis test where applicable, and Student visa application.
AustraliaOffer and CoE, Genuine Student responses, financial evidence, OSHC, health, and character documents.
CanadaLetter of Acceptance, PAL/TAL where required, proof of funds, study plan, biometrics, and medical checks.
New ZealandOffer of Place, funds, insurance, health and character evidence, and a credible study plan.
Japan / KoreaAdmission or language-school documents, financial sponsor proof, health documents, and COE or visa paperwork.

Visa rules, funds, fees, work rights, and required forms can change. Confirm the final checklist on the official immigration website before submission. Aurora can help organize documents and prepare your application, but no visa outcome can be guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions
How early should I start?
Start around four to eight months before your intended intake so there is time for testing, applications, offer conditions, financial preparation, CAS or CoE, and visa processing.
Can I apply with a study gap?
Often yes, when the gap is explained with genuine work, training, family, or other supporting evidence that connects to your current study plan.
What happens after I receive an offer?
Review every condition, pay the required deposit, collect the final enrolment confirmation, check funds and health requirements, then submit the correct visa application.
